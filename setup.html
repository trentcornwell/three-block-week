A weekly planner built for a pastor's life

Every day is Morning, Afternoon and Evening, and each block gets a purpose before it gets a to-do list: study, office, visits, church, family, rest. This page shows what it looks like and walks you through setting up your own private copy. Your staff can join later if you want, but it works great for just you.

  • About 45 minutes
  • Free
  • Private to you
  • No coding, mostly copy and paste
  • Works on phone and computer

What it looks like

Every day is three blocks. Decide what each block is for, then fill your work blocks with objectives and tasks. Sample data

Why it helps a pastor

  • Your family gets planned firstYour day off and family evenings go on the calendar as real blocks, not whatever is left after the church's needs.
  • Purpose before tasksEach block is named before it fills up, so the day has a shape instead of a pile of to-dos.
  • Your study stays protectedSermon prep, reading and seminary work get their own blocks instead of the gaps between meetings and phone calls.
  • Honest guardrailsToo many nights at the church or not enough rest? Your week tells you before your family or your body does.
  • Ministry has a rhythmServices, Bible studies, visits and office hours each have a place, so the week reflects your calling instead of the loudest request.
  • Objectives, not just errands"Finish Sunday's sermon" holds its steps underneath and ties back to your top priorities for the week.
  • Works with your calendarYour Google Calendar events, family calendar included, show up in the right block. Basecamp works too if you use it.
  • Nothing slipsUnfinished items move to the next day with one tap, and a weekly template fills a new week fast.
  • Private and freeYour own copy and your own data. Nobody else can sign in unless you add them. Free hosting, no subscription.
  • Bring your staff laterWhen you're ready, add your staff. Each plans their own week and you see everyone's at a glance.

If something goes wrong

  

    
"That address isn't on the staff list yet"
The planner only lets in addresses on its list. Check the email you added in step 2 matches the one you sign in with exactly. For staff, see "Invite your staff".

    
The sign-in link opens the wrong site or an error page
Check step 5: the Site URL and Redirect URL must match your site address exactly, starting with https://.

    
The page says "Couldn't load the planner"
Check config.js (step 3): the Project URL and publishable key must both be from your Supabase project, inside quotes.

    
Google says "access blocked" or "ineligible accounts"
Add the address as a test user in the Google section. Type it in all lowercase.

    
The planner went quiet after a week of no use
Free Supabase projects pause after about a week without use. Open your Supabase dashboard and click Resume. Daily use keeps it awake.
