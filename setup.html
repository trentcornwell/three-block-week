Run Three-Block Week for your church

A weekly planner for ministry staff: every day is Morning, Afternoon and Evening, and each block gets a purpose before it gets a to-do list. This guide sets up your own private copy, with your own staff list and your own data.

  • About 1 hour
  • Free for a church staff
  • No coding, mostly copy and paste
  • Works on phone and computer

What it looks like

Every day is three blocks. Pick what each block is for, then fill work blocks with objectives and tasks. Sample data

Why pastors use it

  • Rest and family get planned firstSabbath and family evenings are blocks on the calendar, not whatever is left over.
  • Purpose before tasksEach block is named before it fills up, so the day has a shape instead of a pile of to-dos.
  • Study time stays protectedSermon prep and on-going education get their own blocks, not the gaps between meetings.
  • Guardrails that speak upToo many working evenings or too little rest? The week tells you before Sunday does.
  • The whole staff in one viewSee who's in the office, who's resting and what each person is focused on, without a meeting to find out.
  • Objectives, not just errandsBig goals hold their tasks underneath and tie back to the week's top priorities.
  • Works with what you already useGoogle Calendar events show in the right block, and Basecamp to-dos and schedules stay in step.
  • Nothing slipsUnfinished items move to the next day with one tap, and a weekly template fills a new week fast.
  • Private and freeYour own copy, your own data, only your staff can sign in. Free hosting, no subscription.

If something goes wrong

  

    
"That address isn't on the staff list yet"
Add the exact email the person signs in with (step 6). Google and Basecamp sometimes use a different address than people expect.

    
The sign-in link opens the wrong site or an error page
Check step 5: the Site URL and Redirect URL must match your site address exactly, starting with https://.

    
The page says "Couldn't load the planner"
Check config.js (step 3): the Project URL and publishable key must both be from your Supabase project, inside quotes.

    
Google says "access blocked" or "ineligible accounts"
Add the person as a test user (step 7). Type the address in all lowercase.

    
The planner went quiet after a week of no use
Free Supabase projects pause after about a week without use. Open your Supabase dashboard and click Resume. Daily use keeps it awake.
